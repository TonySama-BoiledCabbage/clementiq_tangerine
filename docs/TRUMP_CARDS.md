## A · Safe by the formula, short this month / 公式说安全，本月却付不起

**Claim.** The README runway formula averages balances and medians cash flow, so it cannot see *this month*. We add a check: fixed payments (standing orders + a running loan) vs the latest month-end balance → covered / tight / short.

**Evidence** (full dataset):
- **87 of 228** flagged accounts get the formula's maximum, **120 months** of runway (82 have no monthly gap at all; 5 more have a gap so small the buffer covers 120 months).
- **15** of those 87 are already **short this month**, a combined gap of **$50,047**. Across all 65 short accounts the gap is **$301,463**.
- Demo account **95678**:
  - The formula says runway **120+ (no shortfall)**.
  - This month: fixed payments **$9,961** vs month-end balance **$8,618** → **short $1,343**, and the loan is in arrears (status D).
  - Under EI 55%, the runway is **2.1 months**.
- Second blind spot, average vs month-end balance:
  - **21786**: B (6-month mean daily balance) is **$8,576**, but the month-end balance is **$40,205**.
  - The app says when the two differ by more than 25%.

**Typical entry.** Shows the formula's 120+ and a green badge.

**In the product.**
- Stress Test summary strip: risk flags next to the runway.
- "This month: fixed payments vs balance" opens by itself when the account is short or behind.
- Customer Home: "Right now" card.
- Endpoint: `GET /api/accounts/{id}/obligations`.

**Demo line.** "The formula gives 95678 ten years of runway. Their payments bounce this month."

**Product tie-in.** The check needs the customer's everyday cash flow. That is exactly what a *primary* client brings: Scotiabank defines primary as a daily chequing account plus another payment or investment product.

**Tangerine value.** Fewer credit losses: we catch arrears before default, not after.

**Caveat.**
- Standing orders are the only scheduled-payment signal in the data. Card bills and ad-hoc payments aren't included, so "short" is a floor, not a full picture.
- Say that if asked.

**中文**
- README 公式用的是平均余额和中位数现金流，看不到"这个月"。我们加了一项检查：固定扣款（定期转账加在还的贷款）对比最新月末余额，结果分为够 / 紧 / 不够。
- 87 个在公式里拿到最高分（120 个月）的标记账户里，有 15 个本月已经不够付，缺口合计 $50,047。
- 演示账户 95678：公式显示 120+ 个月；实际上本月扣款 $9,961，余额 $8,618，差 $1,343，贷款已逾期；如果失业领 EI 55%，只撑 2.1 个月。
- 局限：数据里只有定期转账这一种扣款信号，所以"不够付"是保守下限。

## I · Flags line up with loan default / 风险标记与违约相关

**Claim.** Our "short this month or under one month saved" flags line up with which loan holders end up in default.

**Evidence.**
- Among active loan holders, those flagged short or thin defaulted at **41.1%** (30 of 73); everyone else at **7.6%** (46 of 609).
- "Behind" is excluded from the flag set because loan status D is itself a default status, which would make the test circular.

**Demo line.** "Flagged loan holders defaulted at more than five times the rate of everyone else. That's who an advisor should call first."

**Tangerine value.** Fewer credit losses, measured in the bank's own terms.

**Caveat — say it before a judge does.**
- Correlation only. The flags use balances from the end of the data window, the same window in which default status is recorded, so there is time leakage.
- n = 73 is small.
- To claim prediction, recompute using only data from before each loan started.
- Never present this as a credit model; the flags only trigger help (card H).

**中文**
- 被标记为"本月不够付或存款不足 1 个月"的贷款户违约率 41.1%（73 户中 30 户），其他人 7.6%（609 户中 46 户）。
- 已排除"贷款逾期"这个标记，否则就是循环论证。
- 但这只是相关，存在时间泄漏，样本也小。要坐实预测能力，得用贷款发放前的数据重新算。绝不能当信贷模型讲。

## D · Plans people can follow / 真能执行的方案

**Claim.** The plans were designed by simulating an exhausted customer just home from work with a car loan, mortgage and insurance due. The formula didn't drive them. The rules:
- **Lender first.** When a loan is behind or payments exceed the balance, step 1 is "call your lender before the next payment date".
- **No impossible cuts.** If closing the gap needs more than a 60% cut to flexible spending, we don't print it. We say cutting alone can't cover it and point to a deferral and EI.
- **Never green while at risk.** Runway colour can't be green if any flag is on, even at 120+.
- **Small milestone first.** The Goal Plan targets one month of spending, then three. It starts with "stabilise first" when the customer is short or behind.
- **One calm line when stable.** Details expand only when something is wrong.

**Evidence.**
- These rules live in one module (`components/stress/parts.tsx`: `backupSteps`, `runwayTone`, `GoalCard`), shared by the staff and customer screens.
- 95678 shows the lender-first path; 20176 shows the calm one-liner.

**Typical entry.** A fixed list ("cut 20%, build an emergency fund"), regardless of the person.

**Demo line.** "We never tell someone in arrears to save $216 a month. We tell them to call their lender today."

**Tangerine value.** Primacy: advice that respects the customer's situation is why people make a bank their main bank.

**Caveat.** The 60% threshold is a design judgement, not a regulation. Present it that way.

**中文**
- 方案是模拟"下班到家、筋疲力尽、车贷房贷保险同时要交"的客户推出来的，不是从公式推出来的。
- 五条规则：
  - 贷款逾期时，第一步是打给贷款方。
  - 需要削减超过 60% 才能补上缺口时，不给这种削减建议。
  - 有风险时绝不显示绿色。
  - 储蓄目标先攒到 1 个月开销，再到 3 个月。
  - 状态稳定时只显示一行。

## F · Canadian 2026 twist card: mortgage renewal / 2026 加拿大房贷续约冲击

**Claim.** Next to the three README cards, we added the risk most Canadian households face right now: a mortgage renewal at higher payments.

**Evidence** (Bank of Canada staff analytical note, July 2025):
- About 60% of mortgages renewing in 2025–26 see a payment increase.
- The average increase for 2026 renewals is about **6%** across mortgage types.
- Five-year fixed borrowers face about **15–20%**.

Our preset applies **+6%** to the loan payment. The full selector lets you set 20% for a 5-year-fixed story.

**In the product.**
- Stress Test → fourth twist card "Mortgage renewal".
- Customer What if → "My mortgage renews".
- Preset id `mortgage_renewal`.

**Demo line.** "A third of Canadian mortgage holders see a higher payment by the end of 2026. Here's what +6% does to this household."

**Caveat — say it straight.**
- 6% is the average across all mortgage types, not the typical 5-year-fixed hit. TD Economics (March 2026) expects the shock to ease, with payments declining in the second half of 2026 as more borrowers renew at lower rates.
- Don't quote "median near zero": we only found it secondhand, not in TD's own report.
- Say "we can model renewal shocks", not "a renewal crisis is happening".
- The dataset is Czech (PKDD'99), so "mortgage" here means the account's loan payment P.

**中文**
- 在 README 三张卡之外，加了一张"房贷续约"卡。
- 加拿大央行的数据：2025–26 年续约的房贷约 60% 月供上涨；2026 年各类贷款平均约 +6%，5 年固定利率约 +15–20%。
- 我们默认用 +6%，可以手动调到 20%。
- 不要把 6% 说成 5 年固定利率的典型涨幅。

## G · One engine, two products (B2B2C) / 同一引擎，两个产品

**Claim.** Same engine, two front doors, each built from a simulated usage order (what each person opens the tool for, most often first).

| Role | Opens to | First question answered |
|---|---|---|
| Advisor (admin) | Accounts | Who do I call today? |
| Judge | Stress Test | Does this account survive the twist, and is the number right? |
| Customer | Home | Am I OK this month? |

On the Stress Test, the twist card and its result sit on the first screen (summary → twist → result) because that's the judging path. The customer never sees staff pages (role gate + tests).

**Demo line.** "Same numbers, two products: the advisor sees a call list, the customer sees 'you're covered this month'."

**Tangerine value.** A digital bank with no branches needs both: tools for its advisors and self-serve guidance for customers.

## Q&A risks to prepare / Q&A 预案

| Likely question | Answer |
|---|---|
| "This is Czech data." | Yes, PKDD'99 relabelled by the organisers. The Tangerine product mapping is an analogy, not validated on real Tangerine customers. |
| "Is 41% predictive?" | No, it's correlation with time leakage on 73 accounts. Next step: recompute on pre-loan data only. |
| "Isn't this surveillance?" | The only thing a flag does is put someone on a call list for help. It never touches credit decisions, and the API says so. |
| "Tangerine already has Left to Spend and alerts." | Those look back at spending. We look forward, catch what the formula misses, and route each warning into Goals, ASP or an advisor call. We're not a better budgeting tool. |
| "Is the mortgage renewal shock real?" | Yes, but softening: the 2026 average is about +6% (BoC), and TD expects payments to start falling in the second half of 2026. Say "we can model renewal shocks", not "a renewal crisis is happening". |
| "Why 87 and not 82?" | 87 get the formula's maximum score (120 months). 82 of them have no monthly gap at all; the other 5 have a tiny gap and enough savings to cover 120 months. |

**中文** 重点准备：数据是捷克的；41% 只是相关；隐私和监控的观感（用"只触发帮助"回答）；Tangerine 已有类似功能（回答"我们往前看，并接到你们已有的产品上"）；房贷续约冲击在减弱（2026 年平均约 +6%，TD 预计下半年开始下降；说"能模拟"，不说"危机正在发生"；不要引用"中位数接近零"，这个说法找不到原始出处）；为什么是 87 不是 82。
